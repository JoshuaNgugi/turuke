Questions about Turuke?

Who is Turuke for?

Turuke is focused on poultry farmers, starting with egg-laying operations that need a clearer way to manage production, flock records and farm finances.

What can I keep track of?

Core records include flocks, egg production, health events, vaccinations, mortality, egg inventory, sales, expenses and reports.
