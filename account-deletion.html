Account Deletion

We understand you may wish to delete your Turuke account. Please read the information below carefully before submitting your request.

1. How to Delete Your Account

To initiate account deletion, email our support team using the address below. Include the email address or username registered to your Turuke account so we can verify your identity.

Account deletion requests

joshuamwaurangugi@gmail.com

Suggested subject: Turuke Account Deletion Request

We may contact you if we need additional information to verify that you own the account. Please do not send your password.

2. What Data Will Be Deleted?

After we successfully verify your request, the following data associated with your account will be permanently removed from our active databases, subject to any retention required or permitted by law:

  • User profile information: Your name, contact details, and other personal information provided during registration.
  • Flock management data: Records related to your poultry flocks, including flock names, breeds, and numbers.
  • Egg production records: Daily egg counts, production trends, and historical data.
  • Farm performance insights: Reports, analytics, or aggregated data specific to your farm's performance within the app, where linked to your account and capable of deletion.
  • Login credentials: Your username and stored password hash.

3. How Your Data Will Be Handled

  • Permanent deletion: We will initiate deletion after your request has been verified and aim to delete your data from our primary systems within 30 days of confirmation.
  • Backup systems: Residual copies may remain in backup systems for up to 90 days before being purged, in accordance with applicable retention practices and disaster-recovery needs. Backup data is not used for ordinary app activity.
  • Anonymized data: We may retain genuinely anonymized and aggregated data that cannot reasonably be linked back to you for statistical analysis and service improvement.
  • No recovery: Once your account and associated data have been deleted, they cannot be recovered. Please export or otherwise save any information you wish to keep before requesting deletion.
